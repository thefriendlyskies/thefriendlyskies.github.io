When you think about private aviation, you may think of luxurious planes exclusively reserved for billionaires or A-list celebrities. However, private flying is now more attainable, thanks to technological advancements and competitive manufacturing that help meet the increasing demand of affordable private aircraft. Here is a list of the top 10 cheapest planes you can purchase right now.

## 10. HondaJet HA-420

{{< img src="08102026/1.png" id="i_plane" caption="Acroterion / Wikimedia" alt="Honda HA-420 HondaJet N783JT at Frederick Municipal Airport, Maryland, USA.">}}

Starting at the 10th position, the [HondaJet HA-420](https://skybrary.aero/aircraft/hdjt) is a light jet that brings automotive-inspired innovation to the private aviation market. It offers a premium experience at a relatively accessible price point, with pre-owned prices from [\$2.5 to \$3.5 million](https://the-jet-collection.com/private-jets/hondajet-ha-420/price/) depending on model year. Accommodating up to five passengers, Its most recognizable feature is the patented over-the-wing engine mount configuration, eliminating the need for a rear fuselage structure for a cabin 30% more spacious than conventional designs. 

This cheap aircraft is constructed with advanced composite materials and laminar-flow aerodynamics, with a maximum cruise speed of 782 km/h (486 mph) and a maximum range of 2,256 km (1,408 miles). Owner-operators and business executives who are after a blend of cutting-edge technology, efficiency, and comfort are the most common buyers of this model.

## 9. Cirrus Vision Jet (SF50)

{{< img src="08102026/2.png" id="i_plane" caption="JetRequest.com / Wikimedia" alt="Cirrus Vision SF50 exterior facing left.">}}

Introduced in 2015, the [Cirrus Vision Jet SF50](https://cirrusaircraft.com/aircraft/vision-jet/) is an affordable single-engine jet with a parachute safety system. As one of the most affordable jets with jet-level performance, it has a range of 2,222 km (1,381 miles) and a maximum cruise speed of 574.5 km/h (357 mph). It is powered by a single Williams International FJ33-5A turbofan engine mounted on top of the fuselage, with a versatile, modular cabin that typically seats 5 adults.

First-time jet owners and pilots transitioning from piston aircraft tend to appreciate this aircraft’s ease of use and modern technology. Priced around \$1.8 to over \$3.3 million for [pre-owned models](https://www.sf50flightsupport.com/exploring-the-world-of-the-cirrus-sf50-vision-jet/), its operating costs are estimated at \$800 to \$1,000 per hour, making it one of the economical private jets available.

## 8. Embraer Phenom 100

{{< img src="08102026/3.png" id="i_plane" caption="Acroterion / Wikimedia" alt="Embraer Phenom 100 N326N at Frederick Municipal Airport, Maryland, USA.">}}

For regional travels or short business trips, another top pick for an affordable and reliable VLJ is the Embraer Phenom 100\. Many operators prefer it for regional hops and short business trips, combining privacy, comfort, and flexibility. The Phenom comes with Pratt & Whitney PW617F-E engines and a maximum range of 2,182 km (1,356 miles), accommodating  4 or 5 passengers with a maximum cruise speed of roughly 700 km/h (435 mph).

The Phenom 100 has a spacious and comfortable cabin with more windows, allowing more light in, as well as a wide luggage area. Newer models such as the 100EV/100EX are priced between [\$4.5 to \$5.3](https://vref.com/news/details-and-specs-behind-the-embraer-phenom-100/) million, while pre-owned, first-generation models may cost between [\$1.8 to \$3 million](https://www.controller.com/listings/for-sale/embraer/phenom-100/aircraft). It also has a relatively more affordable operating cost compared to other cheap aircraft. 

## 7. Cessna Citation Mustang

{{< img src="08102026/4.png" id="i_plane" caption="RHL Images from England / Wikimedia" alt="G-KLNW Cessna Citation Mustang.">}}

The Cessna Citation Mustang is a popular Very Light Jet (VLJ) ideal for [short-haul business travel](https://thefriendlyskies.net/article/worlds-biggest-private-jet-charter-companies/). Seating 4 to 5 passengers in a club configuration, this aircraft has a two-pilot cockpit and is powered by twin Pratt & Whitney Canada PW615F engines. It has a maximum range of 2,130 km (1,323 miles) and maximum cruise speed of 630 km/h (391 mph).

Many reliable, older, and used models are available in the [\$1.5 to \$2.5 million range](https://www.avbuyer.com/aircraft/private-jets/cessna-citation/mustang), making the Citation Mustang one of the most affordable planes to buy. Owners can also benefit from its low fuel burn and widespread support network. 

## 6. Diamond DA50 RG

{{< img src="08102026/5.png" id="i_plane" caption="Bidgee / Wikimedia" alt="Utility Air (VH-88R) Diamond DA50 RG on display at the 2025 Australian International Airshow.">}}

Combining luxurious design with cutting-edge efficiency, the [Diamond DA50 RG](https://www.diamondaircraft.com/en/private-owners/aircraft/da50/tech-specs/) entered the US market in 2024\. It is powered by a Continental CD300 engine that delivers exceptional fuel economy. While its maximum speed of 335 km/h (208 mph) is modest compared to some rivals, it has a comparable range of 1,520 km (944 miles) with high efficiency.

The DA50 RG is known for its signature gull-wing doors, welcoming passengers to a cabin of hand-stitched leather seats, expansive windows, and intuitive technology. The Garmin G1000 NXi flight deck with synthetic vision pairs with Full Authority Digital Engine Control (FADEC), eliminating mixture and propeller controls for simplified single-lever power management. With a cost typically between [\$1.1 to \$1.3 million](https://www.lifestyleaviation.com/aircraft/diamond/da50), this aircraft sets a new standard for light, high-performance single-engine cheap planes.

## 5. Piper M350 Malibu Mirage

{{< img src="08102026/6.png" id="i_plane" caption="Tomás Del Coro / Wikimedia" alt="N799JP Piper PA 46-350P Malibu Mirage M350.">}}

The [Piper M350 Malibu Mirage](https://www.piper.com/model/m350/) is a pressurized aircraft powered by a turbocharged Lycoming TIO-540-AE2A engine. It is a great choice for pilots transitioning from unpressurized high-performance singles. The M350 can transport up to three people over 2,407 km (1,496 miles) and a maximum speed of 395 km/h (245 mph).

Inside, the Malibu Mirage features premium leather seating, elegant wood and metal trim, and plush carpeting for extra comfort. It also includes practical amenities such as a built-in work table, beverage holders, and individual lighting controls, offering relaxation for passengers at 25,000 feet. Pre-owned Piper M350 aircraft generally cost [between \$410,000 to \$1.9 million](https://www.controller.com/listings/for-sale/piper/malibu-mirage/piston-single-aircraft/6), with newer or lower-time models closer to \$1.6 million.

## 4. Dassault Falcon 50 (Older Model)

{{< img src="08102026/7.png" id="i_plane" caption="Markus Eigenheer from Genève / Wikimedia" alt="N990MM Dassault Breguet Falcon 50 FA50.">}}

​​The [Dassault Falcon 50](https://www.globalair.com/aircraft-specifications/dassault-falcon-jet/falcon-50-specifications/209#:~:text=The%20Dassault%20Falcon%2050%20is,aircraft%20to%20feature%20supercritical%20wings.) is a super-midsize business jet produced by [Dassault Aviation](https://thefriendlyskies.net/article/history-of-private-jets/). Known for its unique tri-jet configuration, this aircraft is powered by three Honeywell TFE731 engines, making it the first private jet with true intercontinental range. Its spacious cabin can typically accommodate eight passengers, and is equipped with supercritical wings for improved performance. 

With a maximum range exceeding 5,556 km (3,452 miles) and a maximum speed of 889 km/h (552 mph), the Falcon 50 remains a capable long-range transport even by modern standards. It is a desirable option in the pre-owned market, with [older models](https://www.controller.com/listings/for-sale/dassault/falcon-50/aircraft) available at as low as \$1.5 million.

## 3. Piper PA-28-140 Cherokee

{{< img src="08102026/8.png" id="i_plane" caption="Bidgee / Wikimedia" alt="Piper PA-28-140 Cherokee Cruiser (VH-PBE) at Wagga Wagga Airport.">}}

Entering the top three cheapest aircraft, the [Piper PA-28-140](https://haltonaeroclub.co.uk/piper-warrior/#:~:text=The%20Piper%20Warrior%20PA%2D28,air%20taxi%20and%20personal%20use.) Cherokee is a dependable four-seat, single-engine aircraft with an all-metal construction and low-wing design. It offers slightly more space and power than two-seat trainers like the Cessna 150\. Powered by a 180-horsepower engine and equipped with tricycle landing gear, it provides a comfortable balance of comfort and affordability, making it ideal for carrying passengers or light cargo, or simply enjoying personal travel. 

With a maximum speed of 228 km/h (142 mph) and a range of 861 km (535 miles), many used models are [available for \$25,000 to \$40,000](https://www.controller.com/listings/for-sale/piper/cherokee-140/aircraft), often having upgraded avionics. Its reasonable operating costs and four-seat capacity positions the Cherokee as an approachable cheap aircraft for pilots seeking more capability without a significant jump in expense or complexity. 

## 2. Cessna 140 

{{< img src="08102026/9.png" id="i_plane" caption="Acroterion / Wikimedia" alt="Cessna 140 N3047N in front of AOPA headquarters, Frederick, Maryland, USA.">}}

Ranking second place of the cheapest planes, the [Cessna 140](https://www.aopa.org/go-fly/aircraft-and-ownership/aircraft-fact-sheets/cessna-120-140) is a classic two-seat, tailwheel light aircraft that first emerged in the late 1940s. Powered by a single Continental C-85 or C-90 engine, it is highly appealing to vintage aviation enthusiasts and student pilots seeking flying experience. This aircraft has a taildragger configuration for short-field performance, ideal for flights from grass strips and smaller airfields. 

As one of the cheapest planes on the market, airworthy models of the Cessna 140 are available for prices ranging from [\$20,000 to \$40,000](https://www.trade-a-plane.com/search?make=CESSNA&model_group=CESSNA+140+SERIES&s-type=aircraft). Its maintenance costs are also remarkably low thanks to its simple system. Although it is not exactly a high-speed aircraft, with a maximum speed of 201 km/h (125 mph), it still makes an accessible and attractive entry point into private aircraft ownership.

## 1. Luscombe 8A

{{< img src="08102026/10.png" id="i_plane" caption="Aeroprints.com / Wikimedia" alt="LV-RGN Luscombe 8A.">}}

Finally, the [Luscombe 8A](https://www.aopa.org/go-fly/aircraft-and-ownership/aircraft-fact-sheets/luscombe-8), often referred to as the "mighty Luscombe," is a classic two-seat, all-metal tailwheel aircraft that has captivated vintage aviation enthusiasts since its 1940s debut. It has a high-wing, sporty design and is notably more agile than contemporaries like the Cessna 140, calling for skilled pilots with a pure flying experience. It is powered by a 65-horsepower Continental A65 engine and offers excellent fuel economy, with a maximum speed of 206 km/h (128 mph) and a range of 805 km (500 miles).

For budget-minded vintage cheap aircraft seekers, the Luscombe 8A is a valuable choice, with airworthy models often available for as little as [\$25,000](https://www.blackjet.com/post/cheap-light-aircraft). Its lightweight all-metal construction also keeps operating costs low while providing durability and minimalist charm for weekend recreational flying or building tailwheel experience.

## What to Consider Before You Buy Cheapest Plane

Before taking the leap into affordable aircraft ownership, it is important to note that purchase price is just the beginning of the financial commitment. Make sure to build realistic budgets that consider both [fixed costs](https://www.blackjet.com/post/private-jet-buy) such as hangar fees (\$2000 to \$6000 monthly), [insurance premiums](https://jettly.com/post/private-jet-operating-cost) (typically 1% to 3% of the aircraft's value annually), and annual inspections. Also, consider [variable expenses](https://www.blackjet.com/post/private-jet-buy) like fuel (\$2,000-5,000 per flight hour) and routine maintenance that may cost \$500-2,000 per flight hour, accumulating funds for major component overhauls.

Annual operating costs for a used aircraft can run as low as [\$12,570 for light users](https://jettly.com/post/affordable-planes), although this is just an estimate and may vary depending on aircraft type and usage. The total cost per hour decreases significantly when the aircraft is flown more frequently, making regular use economically advantageous.

For those shopping in today's market, the used single-engine segment offers remarkable value. Go for aircraft with solid maintenance histories and strong owner communities to ensure their part availability and technical support for the long term. By keeping in mind all these considerations, buyers can find the cheapest planes that offer sustainable, long-term value.
